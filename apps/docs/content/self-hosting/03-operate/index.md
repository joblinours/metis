Once your instance is running, the owner dashboard is where you watch it and change it. These pages cover the dashboard and the jobs around it.

- [Owner dashboard](/self-hosting/operate/owner-dashboard): every page of the dashboard, from usage stats to settings history.
- [Update and roll back](/self-hosting/operate/update-and-roll-back): move to a newer or older release, and what happens when a step fails.
- [Health checks](/self-hosting/operate/health-checks): the endpoints for your uptime monitor or load balancer.
- [Backups](/self-hosting/operate/backups): where your data and secrets live, and what to keep safe.
- [Troubleshooting](/self-hosting/operate/troubleshooting): deploy checks, warnings and common problems.
