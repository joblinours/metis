---
title: Workflows
description: Sort, label and tidy your mail automatically with workflows you build on a canvas.
---

A workflow is a small set of instructions that Metis Suite follows for you. You draw it as a chain of steps: something starts it, it picks up mail, it checks that mail, and then it acts. For example, "when new mail arrives from this sender, archive it" or "every weekday at 9:00, label anything in the inbox that mentions an invoice".

Workflows run in the background on your server, so they keep working when Metis Suite is closed.
