---
title: Calendar
description: See your events, create and change them, and choose which calendars show.
---

The Calendar section shows the events from every calendar account in your workspace in one place. Metis Suite works with Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Zoho Calendar and CalDAV calendars. The ones you can use depend on how your Metis Suite instance is set up. Your events stay with your calendar provider. Metis Suite reads and changes them there.

To add a calendar account to a workspace, see [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces).

- [Views and navigation](/guide/calendar/views-and-navigation): the Day, Week, Month, Year and Schedule views, moving between dates, and time zones.
- [Create an event](/guide/calendar/create-an-event): every way to start a new event, the fields you can fill in, guests and online meetings.
- [View, edit and delete events](/guide/calendar/view-edit-and-delete-events): open an event's details, change it, delete it, or answer an invitation.
- [Move and resize events](/guide/calendar/move-and-resize-events): drag an event to a new time or day, or drag its edge to change how long it lasts.
- [Show and hide calendars](/guide/calendar/show-and-hide-calendars): hide a calendar for yourself, and choose which calendars a workspace shows.
- [Calendar AI](/guide/calendar/calendar-ai): open the assistant beside your calendar and ask it about your events.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/calendar/keyboard-shortcuts): switch views, move between dates and create events from the keyboard.
