---
title: Drive
description: Browse, search, upload, preview and manage the files in the drives connected to your workspace.
---

The Drive section shows the files in the drives your workspace can use. A drive is one connected storage account, such as a Google Drive, a OneDrive, or a storage server your administrator set up. Your files stay in that account. Metis shows them and makes your changes there.

- [Browse your files](/guide/drive/browse-your-files): switch drives, use list and grid views, move through folders, and select and open items.
- [All drives](/guide/drive/all-drives): see every drive in the workspace in one place.
- [Search](/guide/drive/search): find a file by name, and how search works on each drive type.
- [Upload files](/guide/drive/upload-files): add files with the Upload button or by dragging them in, and the limits that apply.
- [Create, rename and delete](/guide/drive/create-rename-and-delete): make folders, rename items, and know when a delete can be undone.
- [Download and open at the provider](/guide/drive/download-and-open): save a file to your computer or open it in Google Drive or OneDrive.
- [Preview files](/guide/drive/preview-files): which files open in the preview pane, the size limits, and which drives support it.
- [Pinned and recent folders](/guide/drive/pinned-and-recent-folders): keep folders one click away in the sidebar.
- [Folder restrictions](/guide/drive/folder-restrictions): choose which top-level folders of a drive a workspace can see.
- [Add a drive](/guide/drive/add-a-drive): connect a drive account and add it to a workspace.
- [Drive types](/guide/drive/drive-types): what differs between Google Drive, OneDrive, S3, WebDAV, FTP and SFTP.
- [Drive AI](/guide/drive/drive-ai): ask the assistant about the files in a drive.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/drive/keyboard-shortcuts): every Drive shortcut in one table.

To save a mail attachment to a drive, or to link a drive file in an email, see [Mail](/guide/mail).
